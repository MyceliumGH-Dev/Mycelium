# Mycelium Icon System — design brief

Instructions for the design agent drawing the Mycelium component icons. Modelled on
the Eddy3D v4 vector set (`Eddy3D/GUI/Icons/icons/v4/`), which solved the same problem
for 114 Grasshopper components; read that set's `README.txt` and `src/eddy-vec.js` first
if you want a worked precedent.

The point of this document is that Mycelium's 11 glyphs must read as **one set**, not as
11 independent drawings. That is achieved structurally — a shared engine applies weight,
colour and construction — not by an agent trying to remember the rules per glyph.

---

## 1. What exists today, and what is wrong with it

The current icons live in `src/Mycelium/Icons/*.png` as 24×24 PNGs generated by an
ad-hoc Pillow script (no source retained). They are serviceable but not a system:

| Problem | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Mixed projection | `MyceliumCourtyard`, `MyceliumL`, `MyceliumU`, `MyceliumLinear`, `MyceliumPoint` are plan views; `MyceliumTower`, `MyceliumTree`, `MyceliumTerrain` are elevations. The eye cannot tell whether a glyph describes a footprint or a mass. |
| Plugin mark collides with a component | `Mycelium.png` (skyline on roots) and `MyceliumMassing.png` (bars on roots) are near-duplicates at 24 px. The assembly mark must be distinct from every component. |
| Typologies under-differentiated | `MyceliumCourtyard` and `MyceliumPoint` are both concentric squares, separated only by the inner shape's size. |
| No shared weight or ink | Stroke thickness and fill ratio vary glyph to glyph; there is no defined ink colour. |
| Generic metaphor | `MyceliumTemplate` is a plain document page — it says "file", not "fetch a versioned example definition from GitHub". |
| No vector source | Only rasters ship. Any future retina/32 px/48 px need means redrawing from scratch. |

Fixing the projection inconsistency and the plugin/Massing collision is the primary
brief. Everything else follows from adopting the rules below.

---

## 2. Deliverables and layout

Author into `design/icons/`; ship only the PNGs.

```
src/Mycelium/Icons/*.png       SHIPPED. 24x24 PNG, transparent. Embedded by the
                               csproj glob <EmbeddedResource Include="Icons\*.png" />.
                               Nothing but rasters belongs here.
design/icons/
  README.md                    this brief
  manifest.csv                 glyph name, component, ribbon panel, family, accent
  svg/                         one standalone 24x24 SVG per glyph — the vector source
  Mycelium_Icons_Vector.svg    sprite sheet, one <symbol id="myc-NAME"> per glyph
  src/myc-vec.js               the engine: motif library M, badge set B, safe-area guard
  src/myc-vec-set.js           one def() per glyph, grouped by ribbon panel
  spec.html                    contact sheet at 24 / 48 / 96 px on light and dark — open in a browser
```

Both the SVG and the PNG must be emitted from the same `V.glyph(def)` call so the
safe-area guard runs on every glyph. Rasterise by drawing the 24×24 SVG into a 24×24
canvas — **never** draw at 96 px and downscale; the 1.25-unit stroke has to land on the
pixel grid, and a downscale turns it to mush.

---

## 3. The rules

**Space.** 24 × 24 units, `viewBox="0 0 24 24"`. All art inside `0.7 .. 23.3`. The guard
in the engine must fail the build on any path outside that box.

**Stroke.** Exactly two weights, no others:
- `1.25` units — every contour.
- `0.85` units — interior hairlines only (floor plates, parcel divisions, grid rules,
  contour lines, branch veins).

Round caps, round joins. Ink is **pine `#29473A`**.

**Fill.** White for built volume, with grey shaded faces where a mass needs a readable
third dimension. The family accent fills what grows, flows, or is ground. The assembly
mark is the sole monochrome exception: near-black `#202020` and white only, so the brand
remains clear at favicon, website, and Grasshopper ribbon sizes.

**Tinting.** A filled detail narrower than about 3.5 units gets stroked in its own colour
instead of ink — a 1.25 contour on both sides would eat it.

**Effects.** None. No gradient, no shadow, no blur, no rounded-rect card behind the glyph.

**Projection — decide once and hold it.** Every glyph that shows a building mass uses the
same axonometric: **30° isometric, viewer above-left, vertical extrusion up**. Plan views
are reserved for glyphs whose subject genuinely *is* a footprint operation (parcel
subdivision). Do not mix within a family. This is the single biggest change from the
current set: the five typology configs become small isometric masses, not plan outlines.

**Composition.** One primary motif, plus at most one badge. Badges sit bottom-right,
centred at `(17, 17)`, radius `4.4`, on cream `#F1EDE1` so they punch out of the motif.
Use a badge only to say *what the component does to* the motif — `gear` for "configure",
`down` for "fetch", `grid` for "subdivide". A motif that needs two badges means the
motif is wrong.

## 4. Colour means what the component acts on

Hue encodes subject matter, not ribbon position. Four families, from the Mycelium brand
palette (variant A4, locked 2026-07-08):

| Family | Accent | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `built` | pine `#29473A` | building typologies, massing — accent equals ink, so these read as ink-and-white line drawings |
| `plant` | sage `#7E9469` | trees, parks, vegetation |
| `ground` | soil `#6B4F35` | terrain, parcels, site |
| `tool` | slate `#5A6660` | templates, utilities, diagnostics |

Cream `#F1EDE1` is not a family; it is the badge ground and the only permitted opaque
backdrop. `slate` is a derived neutral, not a brand colour — it exists because tooling
glyphs must recede next to the content glyphs. If you find a fifth subject that needs its
own hue, raise it rather than reaching for an off-palette colour.

## 5. The file name is the wiring — and here it is explicit

Mycelium differs from Eddy3D on this point, and the difference matters.

Eddy3D derives the resource name from the component's display name, so a one-character
drift silently unwires the icon. Mycelium instead names the resource **explicitly in the
component**:

```csharp
protected override Bitmap Icon => ComponentIcons.Get("MyceliumCourtyard");
```

`ComponentIcons.Get` resolves `Mycelium.Icons.<name>.png` off the assembly's manifest and
**returns null on a miss** (`src/Mycelium/ComponentIcons.cs:22`) — Grasshopper then draws
its default box, and nothing errors, in any build or test. So:

- Keep the `Mycelium` prefix on every glyph; it is the manifest-resource namespace convention here.
- **Do not rename an existing glyph** for tidiness. If a name must change, change the
  string literal in the component in the same commit.
- A new component needs its `ComponentIcons.Get("…")` argument and the PNG base name to
  match exactly, case included.

The eleven names are fixed by `manifest.csv` in this directory. Treat that file as the
contract: every row must have a glyph, every glyph must have a row.

## 6. Per-glyph brief

Panels come from the ribbon layout (tab `Mycelium`; panels Massing / Building Types /
Vegetation / Site / Utilities).

| Glyph | Component | Family | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Mycelium` | assembly mark (`MyceliumInfo.Icon`) | built | The brand mark — a compact low-/mid-rise urban morphology connected to Grasshopper-like mycelial data wires and endpoint nodes. It is strictly monochrome, must be legible at 16 px in the ribbon tab, and shares **no** silhouette with `MyceliumMassing`. |
| `MyceliumMassing` | Massing Generator | ground | The generator, not the result: a parcel field being subdivided and extruded. Suggest plan-to-mass — subdivided footprints at the base, one or two masses risen. `grid` badge. |
| `MyceliumCourtyard` | Courtyard Config | built | Isometric ring of mass around a void. The void is the subject; keep it wide enough to survive 24 px. `gear` badge. |
| `MyceliumLinear` | Linear Config | built | One long isometric bar, clearly longer than it is deep. `gear` badge. |
| `MyceliumPoint` | Point Config | built | A single compact isometric block, roughly square in plan. Must not read as a small courtyard — no interior void at all. `gear` badge. |
| `MyceliumL` | L-Shape Config | built | Isometric L. Orient the two legs so the corner faces the viewer; a plan-view L is what we are moving away from. `gear` badge. |
| `MyceliumU` | U-Shape Config | built | Isometric U, opening toward the viewer's lower-right so the opening is visible rather than hidden by the near wall. `gear` badge. |
| `MyceliumTower` | Tall Building Config | built | Isometric tower — tall, slender, with `0.85` floor-plate hairlines to say "many storeys". This is the one typology where verticality is the whole message. `gear` badge. |
| `MyceliumTree` | Tree Config | plant | A single tree in the same isometric world as the masses, so it sits beside them credibly. Sage canopy, soil trunk. `gear` badge. |
| `MyceliumTerrain` | Terrain Generator | ground | Displaced ground surface with `0.85` contour hairlines. Not a mountain-range pictogram — this component makes a noise-displaced mesh, so show a subtly undulating field. |
| `MyceliumTemplate` | Mycelium Templates | tool | Fetching a versioned example definition from GitHub. Stacked definition cards with a `down` badge — hand-off and download, not "document". Carries no GitHub mark; third-party logos stay out of Mycelium artwork. |

Note the family reassignments: `MyceliumMassing` moves from built to `ground` (it operates
on parcels), and `MyceliumTerrain` stays `ground`. The five typology configs plus the
brand mark are `built`.

## 7. Verification before you hand off

1. **Safe area** — the guard in `V.glyph` must pass for all 11 glyphs, no exceptions list.
2. **Size** — every shipped PNG is exactly 24 × 24 and a real PNG. Check the IHDR, not the
   filename.
3. **Manifest parity** — every glyph in `svg/` has a `manifest.csv` row and vice versa.
4. **Wiring** — for each of the 11 names, a `ComponentIcons.Get("<name>")` call exists in
   `src/Mycelium/` (10 components + `MyceliumInfo`), or the glyph is orphaned.
5. **Contact sheet** — open `spec.html` and confirm at 24 px that no two glyphs are
   confusable, especially Point/Courtyard and Mycelium/Massing, and that nothing
   disappears against Grasshopper's light-grey canvas.

Mycelium has no test project. Eddy3D's `Icons.Tests/TestIconCoverage.cs` is the reference
implementation for checks 2–4 and is worth porting once the set stabilises; until then
these are manual gates and the contact sheet is the real review artefact.

---

## 8. Scope discipline

If a component cannot be expressed as one motif from `M` plus at most one badge from `B`,
the vocabulary needs a considered addition to the engine — a new motif every other glyph
can also use. It does not need a one-off drawing. That distinction is what keeps a set of
11 from becoming 11 unrelated pictures, and it is the lesson the Eddy3D set exists to
encode.
